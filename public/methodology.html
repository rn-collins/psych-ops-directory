Methodology and evidence boundaries

This deployment is a read-only editorial snapshot, not a marketplace or recommendation engine. Records identify organizations that appeared in a prior field-mapping corpus. Duplicate names are removed from the public payload.

The legacy corpus does not contain claim-level public provenance. Therefore this release withholds pricing, credentials, rankings, acquisitions, promotional characterizations, and detailed service claims. Every displayed record is marked provenance pending.

Future detailed claims require an evidence record containing the exact proposition, primary URL, publisher, source type, publication and access dates, jurisdiction, reviewer, verification result, boundary, and next-review date.

“Tier” labels are retired because they could imply endorsement or quality. Categories are descriptive only.
