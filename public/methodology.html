Methodology and evidence boundaries

This is a read-only editorial snapshot, not a marketplace or a recommendation engine. Each record names an organization that appeared in an earlier survey of the field. Duplicate names are removed before publication, so the published count is lower than the number of raw entries.

That survey did not record a public source for each individual claim. This release therefore withholds pricing, credentials, rankings, acquisitions, promotional characterizations, and detailed service claims, and every record is marked provenance pending.

Future detailed claims require an evidence record containing the exact proposition, primary URL, publisher, source type, publication and access dates, jurisdiction, reviewer, verification result, boundary, and next-review date.

“Tier” labels are retired because they could imply endorsement or quality. Categories are descriptive only.

Dates and links

Entries were last reviewed on 15 August 2026. Destination links were separately checked on 29 August 2026: each was requested over HTTPS, and only those that answered are published as links.

Fourteen entries are shown without a link — thirteen whose domain did not resolve or served no working HTTPS site on that date, and one that never had a website recorded. They are listed rather than deleted, because a name appearing in the field is the fact this index records; a working website is not. Sites that refuse automated requests but serve people normally are treated as reachable.

Neither date is generated from the clock. Both are recorded values, and they will only change when the work is done again.
