Corrections and removal

Fastest route: flag the record

Open the relevant listing in the directory and select Flag. Identify what is inaccurate, outdated, unsafe, duplicated, closed, or no longer authorized. An email address is optional.

Open the directory

Removal, privacy, or identity-sensitive requests

Email rncollins@antithesis.law with the listing ID, organization name, requested action, supporting source, and your relationship to the record. Do not send confidential client information.

Editorial handling

Requests enter the same review queue as public flags. The editor checks the cited source, current website or regulator record where applicable, and the directory’s tier criteria before correcting, downgrading, annotating, or removing the listing. A listing may be temporarily marked unverified while a material dispute is reviewed.

Scope

Directory tiers describe public evidence of field presence. They are not licensure, quality, safety, financial-stability, or endorsement determinations. Users must independently verify a provider before relying on it.
